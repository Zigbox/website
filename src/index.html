Projects

A box, and a cube to put boxes in

Two projects, both written in Zig with nothing but its standard library, and both developed in the open. Zigbox is in public beta; Zigcube is pre-alpha.

Zigbox

container runtime · image store · builder

Rootless and daemonless. Images are Merkle trees of BLAKE3-hashed files, builds are sandboxed and reproducible, and every refusal says what it refused and why.

$ zbx pull alpine $ zbx run alpine sh # no daemon, no root
Status and demos →Source

Zigcube

orchestrator for Zigbox workloads

It runs only what it can verify. Nodes swap images as chunks and check each against its digest, the control plane can be simulated a seed at a time, and it can tell you why each replica is where it is.

$ zcube apply hello.zon $ zcube why service hello # every decision has a cause
